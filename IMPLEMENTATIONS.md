## Listing criteria

An implementation is listed when it: (1) is publicly available under an OSI-approved or
source-available license, (2) states which spec version it targets, and (3) once the conformance
corpus is published, passes it for that version. Until the corpus exists, listings are marked
"pending". To be added, open a pull request.
