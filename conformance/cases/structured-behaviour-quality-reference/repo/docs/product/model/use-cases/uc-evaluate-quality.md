## Goal

Evaluate an observable quality obligation.

## Trigger

The evaluator starts a quality check.

## Preconditions

The quality obligation is available.

## Main Flow

1. The evaluator performs the check.
2. The evaluator records the result.

## Alternative Flows

None.

## Failure Conditions

The check cannot be completed.

## Postconditions

The result is available.
