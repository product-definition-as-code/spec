## Purpose

Reviews what the product recorded about past activity.

## Goals

Obtain an accurate account of recorded activity for a chosen period.

## Responsibilities

Request an export, check it against the period requested and raise discrepancies.

## Boundaries

Does not change recorded activity and does not decide retention policy.
