## Responsibility

Establishes what it means for a repository or an implementation to satisfy the product definition, and the language in which that claim is made.

## Language

Inside this context, a claim of conformance is always a claim about observable behaviour measured against a fixed set of cases. Terms such as fixture, case and expectation carry the meanings defined here and not the meanings a delivery process gives them.

## Boundaries

This context covers the vocabulary of conformance claims. It does not cover how an implementation is built, how a claim is published, or how a reader decides whether the claim matters to them.

## External Relationships

Consumers of a conformance claim read it from outside this context. Nothing outside decides what the terms mean.
