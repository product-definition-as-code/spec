## Rule

Consumer documents MUST cite canonical product text through the citation contract; they MUST NOT
re-state it.

## Rationale

Re-statement is the gap PDaC exists to close.

## Examples

An SDD spec cites `FR-X#S1` instead of copying the scenario text.

## Exceptions

None.
