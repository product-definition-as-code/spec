## Rule

The Product Definition MUST NOT be modified except by applying an approved Product Change that is then accepted through human review.

## Rationale

Silent edits break trust in the canonical model.

## Examples

A tool applying or accepting a model change without human approval violates this rule.

## Exceptions

None.
