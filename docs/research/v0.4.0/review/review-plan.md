# Independent documentary review

The owner authorizes documentary review using existing tools/subscriptions. Monetary/token ceilings, billing checks and usage metering are **not prerequisites**. No purchases or paid infrastructure. Review is useful parallel evidence, not a prerequisite for exploratory computation. Scored cohort claims still require independent evaluation and a frozen protocol.

Use fresh reviewer sessions without authoring history or memory; reviewers do not author their reviewed documents. The [original snapshot manifest](snapshot-manifest.json) preserves the thirteen original files across #141–148. Also inspect #149's consolidated working revision and explicitly record that administrative correction and new experiments differ from the original packet. Record exact exposure and conflicts, requested/resolved/provider-reported model identities where available; unknown fields stay unknown. Different sessions/models are not independent human expert validation and do not eliminate common-provider errors.

Three tracks: [scientific](scientific.md), [methodology](methodology.md), [architecture](architecture.md). Each report gives coverage, exact claim locations, primary-source or reproducible evidence, severity, certainty, minimal correction and limitations. P0: fabricated evidence/false proof/concealed product decision. P1: material guarantee, methodology or compatibility error. P2: reproducibility or limited-scope clarity defect. P3: minor evidenced correction. No speculative enhancements or scope expansion.

Read-only source/primary-source inspection; no recursive reviewers, purchases, production changes or automatic merges. Keep reviews focused; stop rather than loop on inaccessible sources. Report unreviewed areas. Coordinator preserves original findings, records dispositions and exact corrected revisions, reconciles dependent documents and requests proportionate re-review for materially changed claims. Routine bounded corrections are authorized; no separate remediation budget gate.

Documentary critique does not adjudicate product intent or held-out outcomes. Human acceptance, future evaluator/custodian assignments and scientific comparison freeze remain unresolved. No scored phase transition follows review automatically.
