Read-only source inspection only. No solver execution, paid services, edits to source PRs, messages, recursive agents or scope expansion. Stop at the approved resource cap or access/billing uncertainty; record incomplete review rather than retry or hide gaps. The coordinator, not the reviewer, resolves and reconciles findings under separately approved allowances.
