## Definition

One booking occupies one indivisible room slot when confirmed. Capacity and used slots are mathematical integers; lead time is measured in whole days. A requested booking does not occupy a slot.

## Distinguish From

The aggregate number of used slots includes other bookings. Negative lead times are excluded as a separate research assumption, not inferred from the lead-time wording.

## Usage

This meaning is local to the draft scheduling context.
